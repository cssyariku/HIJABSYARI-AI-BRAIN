## GOVERNANCE

Do not add strategic changes solely because an AI “prefers” them.

A meaningful change should be triggered by at least one:
- explicit user decision;
- new account analytics;
- credible research;
- operational constraint;
- repeated audience evidence.

When changing MASTER-level strategy:
1. record reason here;
2. update `MASTER.md`;
3. update `CURRENT_STATE.md`;
4. check dependent prompts/content system;
5. bump version.
