## 1. Audience problem
Outfit hitam mudah dipakai, tetapi banyak orang tetap bingung memilih warna hijab selain hitam yang terasa aman, rapi, dan tidak membuat tampilan terlalu berat.

## 2. Core promise
Satu outfit hitam bisa berubah nuansa hanya dengan mengganti warna hijab.

## 3. Hook variants
1. Baju hitam nggak harus selalu dipasangkan dengan hijab hitam.
2. Pakai outfit hitam? Coba 5 warna hijab ini.
3. Outfit hitam kamu terasa monoton? Ganti warna hijabnya.
4. Satu baju hitam, 5 vibe berbeda.
5. Kalau bajumu hitam, mulai dari 5 warna hijab ini.

**Selected hook:** “Satu baju hitam, 5 vibe berbeda.”

Alasan: spesifik, visual, mudah dipahami dalam 1–2 detik, dan cocok untuk format transisi warna.

## 4. Reel structure
Target duration: **12–15 detik**.

### Scene 1 — 0.0–1.5s
Visual: model perempuan Muslim dewasa memakai outfit hitam polos, framing medium/full body, latar editorial netral.  
On-screen text: **Satu baju hitam, 5 vibe berbeda.**

### Scene 2 — 1.5–3.5s
Hijab: **Cream / ivory**  
On-screen text: **Cream — clean & lembut**  
Direction: terang, ringan, effortless.

### Scene 3 — 3.5–5.5s
Hijab: **Taupe / mushroom**  
On-screen text: **Taupe — netral & polished**  
Direction: muted neutral, modern daily look.

### Scene 4 — 5.5–7.5s
Hijab: **Mocha / cocoa**  
On-screen text: **Mocha — hangat & grounded**  
Direction: warm neutral, elegant but approachable.

### Scene 5 — 7.5–9.5s
Hijab: **Dusty rose / muted mauve**  
On-screen text: **Dusty rose — soft contrast**  
Direction: subtle feminine contrast without becoming too bright.

### Scene 6 — 9.5–11.5s
Hijab: **Burgundy / deep wine**  
On-screen text: **Burgundy — rich & statement**  
Direction: deeper contrast, dressier impression.

### Scene 7 — 11.5–14.0s
Visual: fast 5-look recap, same model/outfit/pose.  
On-screen text: **Kamu pilih yang mana?**  
Secondary text: **Simpan buat referensi outfit hitam.**

## 5. Shot list
Use the same:
- adult Southeast Asian/Indonesian-presenting Muslim woman;
- black modest outfit;
- camera angle;
- pose;
- background;
- lighting;
- hijab style and fabric family.

Only the hijab color changes. This keeps the comparison visually valid and reduces confounders for EXP-003/005.

## 6. Visual direction
- clean editorial;
- premium but approachable;
- neutral studio or minimal real interior;
- realistic textile detail;
- natural skin texture;
- no visible brand logos;
- no text generated inside AI image;
- modest styling;
- no exposed hair;
- avoid excessive jewelry;
- maintain exact subject identity across all five variants.

## 7. Base image prompt
Create a premium but approachable Indonesian modest-fashion editorial portrait of an adult Southeast Asian Muslim woman wearing a clean black modest outfit and a neatly styled opaque hijab. Medium-to-full body framing, natural posture, soft neutral editorial interior, realistic skin texture, believable fabric folds, contemporary modest styling, subtle soft daylight, uncluttered composition, negative space for mobile text overlay, no logos, no text, no watermark. Keep the subject identity, outfit, pose, framing, background and lighting consistent across all variants.

## 8. Color variants
Generate the same image with only the hijab color changed to:
1. warm cream / ivory;
2. taupe / mushroom;
3. mocha / cocoa brown;
4. muted dusty rose / mauve;
5. burgundy / deep wine.

## 9. Negative prompt
uncanny face, plastic skin, waxy texture, over-smoothed face, malformed hands, extra fingers, fused fingers, distorted wrists, duplicate jewelry, floating fabric, impossible folds, clothing clipping, warped background, random text, fake logo, watermark, exposed hair, excessive makeup, doll-like face, unrealistic body proportions, hyper-saturated AI look, inconsistent identity, inconsistent lighting, color shift outside hijab, garment mutation.

## 10. Image-to-video prompt
Animate each editorial still as a subtle 1.5–2 second fashion clip. Preserve exact facial identity, body proportions, black outfit, hijab style, hijab color and background. Use only natural micro movement: slight breathing, minimal head/eye movement, subtle fabric motion and gentle camera push-in. Do not morph the face, change clothing, reveal hair, alter hijab color, add accessories, distort hands or warp the background.

## 11. Edit notes
- 9:16 vertical.
- Keep cuts synchronized to the hijab-color changes.
- Avoid flashy transition effects; use clean cuts or short dissolves.
- Keep headline large enough for mobile.
- Do not place a small “Color Lab” label above the main headline.
- Use brand logo only if it does not compete with the hook.
- Audio should support pacing but content must remain understandable muted.
