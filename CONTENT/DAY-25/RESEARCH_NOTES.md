# DAY 25 — Research Notes

**Accessed:** 31 October 2026  
**Scope:** claim boundaries for textile names, fiber composition, and care information.

## Verified facts

1. Textile fiber content and percentages are distinct product information. The U.S. Federal Trade Commission's Textile Act guidance explains that covered products list generic fiber names and percentages by weight. This supports telling readers to inspect actual composition rather than inferring it from a market-facing fabric name.
2. A fabric may be woven, knitted, felted, or otherwise made from fiber/yarn. Construction and fiber identity are separate concepts.
3. Care instructions are product-specific and should state the regular care needed, with warnings where a plausible care method may harm the item. This supports a care-label-first recommendation instead of universal wash/iron advice.
4. Textile properties depend on multiple design choices. NC State's textile education overview explicitly treats fiber/yarn selection, fabric type, construction, and end-use properties as related variables.

## Excluded claims

- No claim that a market name guarantees fiber composition.
- No universal breathability, heat, slip, opacity, care, durability, or comfort ranking.
- No current-trend, popularity, product-review, testimonial, or purchase recommendation claim.
