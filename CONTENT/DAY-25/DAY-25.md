## Objective

Membantu audience memilih hijab berdasarkan karakter yang dibutuhkan—struktur, drape, stretch, sheen, texture, coverage, dan perawatan—bukan hanya berdasarkan nama bahan di etalase.

## Audience problem

Nama seperti voal, chiffon, jersey, satin, atau crepe sering dipakai sebagai jalan pintas saat belanja. Namun dua produk dengan nama pasar serupa dapat terasa dan tampil berbeda karena komposisi serat, konstruksi, berat, ketebalan, dan finishing. Audience membutuhkan kerangka keputusan yang dapat dipakai ulang.

## KPI / EXP-026

**Hypothesis:** a decision framework plus pre-purchase checklist produces stronger saves and useful replies than a material comparison that is only descriptive.  
**Variable:** five character-based material directions plus four-question chooser.  
**Control:** a descriptive material carousel of comparable visual quality, if tested later.  
**Primary KPI:** saves and save rate when Insights are available.  
**Secondary KPI:** shares, carousel completion, comments/replies naming an activity or preferred character, profile visits, Poll participation, and Questions replies.  
**Limitations:** material names, fiber composition, construction, weight, finishing, display settings, and individual comfort preferences vary.

No result is recorded until actual publication and Insights exist.

## Claim boundaries

- Material families are described with conditional, observable tendencies—not guarantees.
- “Voal-like”, “chiffon-like”, and “satin-surface” intentionally describe visual/construction directions, not verified fiber composition.
- Product composition and care instructions must be checked on the actual label or seller specification.
- No current trend, product performance, breathability, temperature, hypoallergenic, or universal comfort claim is made.
