# DAY 30 — AI Prompt Record

## ImageGen source prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: controlled editorial 2-by-2 source image for four Indonesian Instagram Stories asking the audience to vote on next month's hijab topics.

Create one highly realistic premium editorial 2-by-2 contact sheet with four equal portrait panels and narrow warm-ivory gutters. Show the exact same adult Southeast Asian Muslim woman, age 28–35, with identical realistic facial identity, natural skin tone, cocoa opaque hijab, loose warm-stone long-sleeve abaya, camera quality, and soft daylight in all four panels.

- Panel 1 — Hijab stay: at a mirror, gently checking the front edge and shoulder drape after movement.
- Panel 2 — Color pairing: comparing muted-sage and dusty-rose opaque hijab swatches beside a cocoa-brown outfit.
- Panel 3 — Material choice: examining the weave and sewn care label of a separate taupe hijab.
- Panel 4 — Face framing: evaluating a soft clean frame at a mirror while preserving her face shape.

All garments are loose and opaque; hair, ears, neck, shoulders, and chest remain fully covered. Preserve realistic skin, hands, anatomy, fabric, labels, mirror reflections, and garment rail. Keep clean negative space in the upper third for deterministic typography.

## Negative constraints

No embedded text, letters, numbers, logos, brands, watermark, packaging, charts, metrics, testimonials, childlike model, exposed hair or neck, tight or transparent clothing, malformed hands, extra fingers, duplicated limbs, floating fabric, impossible folds, inconsistent identity, face slimming, glamour pose, plastic skin, distress, clutter, or hyper-saturated AI styling.

## Composition notes

- Text and interaction cards are rendered deterministically in `render.py`.
- Native Instagram Poll and Questions stickers are added only at upload.
- The generated source is editorial visualization, not evidence of actual respondents, preferences, or an office team.
