# DAY 32 — AI Prompt Record

## ImageGen final prompt

**Use case:** photorealistic-natural  
**Asset type:** source editorial triptych for an Instagram hijab-care carousel

Create one cohesive horizontal three-panel editorial photograph showing the same adult Southeast Asian Muslim woman (late 20s) carefully storing hijabs at home.

1. She checks that a taupe hijab is clean and fully dry on a clean table.
2. Close view of her placing three neatly folded hijabs in a shallow drawer with broad, softly rounded folds and no sharp compression.
3. She stores a lightweight hijab as a loose roll in a breathable cream fabric organizer, with no tight rubber bands or metal pins.

Calm modern bedroom wardrobe, warm ivory and muted taupe palette, uncluttered. Highly realistic natural editorial photography, premium but approachable. Three equal side-by-side vertical panels in one wide image; same model identity; modest cream long-sleeve abaya; taupe hijab; natural hands visible; enough independent crop space. Soft diffused daylight, neutral white balance. Realistic woven fibres, fabric organizer, wood drawer, gravity, and folds.

Adult Muslim woman; fully covered hair, neck, chest, and shoulders; anatomically correct hands; realistic fabric; no typography, logo, or watermark.

Avoid exposed hair or neck, tight clothing, plastic skin, malformed or extra fingers, floating cloth, impossible folds, transparent coverage, metal pins left in fabric, rubber bands, mothballs, plastic vacuum bags, clutter, jewelry, fake text.

## Reuse boundary

- Editorial visualization only; not proof that one organizer or method preserves every hijab.
- Do not reuse for claims about durability, mould prevention, wrinkle elimination, or product performance.
- Crop panels independently but do not alter textile colour or actions in ways that change the care guidance.
