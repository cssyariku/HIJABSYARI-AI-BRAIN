## Objective

Memberi lima arah warna hijab yang mudah dibandingkan untuk outfit cokelat: warm cream, dusty rose, muted olive, dusty blue, dan burgundy. Konten membantu audience memilih berdasarkan nuansa dan tingkat kontras, bukan memberi aturan warna universal.

## Audience problem / desire

Outfit cokelat sering terasa aman, tetapi audience dapat bingung apakah hijab sebaiknya tetap tonal, dibuat lebih ringan, diberi aksen warna lembut, atau dibuat lebih dalam. Perbandingan terkontrol memudahkan keputusan tanpa harus membeli warna baru terlebih dahulu.

## KPI / EXP-024

**Hypothesis:** A controlled five-color comparison plus a three-question chooser will earn stronger saves and specific color-preference comments than a single brown-outfit look.  
**Variable:** five named color directions with identical model/outfit/pose/lighting.  
**Control:** single-look brown outfit inspiration of comparable quality, if tested later.  
**Primary KPI:** saves and save rate, when Insights are available.  
**Secondary KPI:** shares, carousel completion, comments naming 1–5, profile visits, Story Poll participation, and Questions replies.  
**Limitations:** screen calibration, ambient light, fabric texture, editing, posting time, and audience composition can change perceived color and response.

No result is recorded until actual publication and Insights exist.
