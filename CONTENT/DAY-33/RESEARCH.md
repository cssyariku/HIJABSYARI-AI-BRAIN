# DAY 33 — Research Notes

**Checked:** 12 November 2026  
**Scope:** textile variability, product-information boundary, and separation of fact from styling hypothesis.

## Facts used

- Fiber content, yarns, construction, and surface treatment/finishing can affect a textile’s texture and behavior.
- Textile labels and product information are the appropriate reference for fiber content and care instructions when available.
- Therefore the package does not claim that one named material, inner, pin, magnet, or technique will stay identically on every wearer.

## Editorial observations / hypotheses

- The starting cheek edge, number of stacked layers, direction of pull, and attachment point are visible styling variables that can be compared.
- The “menoleh · bicara · senyum” sequence is an editorial movement check proposed for self-observation; it is not a standardized or validated test.
- A flatter edge and fewer stacked layers are optional starting points, not corrections for a face shape.

## Release boundary

- No current trend, viral hack, product recommendation, clinical result, or performance metric is claimed.
