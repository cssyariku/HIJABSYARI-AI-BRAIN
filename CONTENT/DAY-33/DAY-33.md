## Objective

Memberi tiga titik pemeriksaan saat tepi hijab di area pipi mudah bergeser: garis awal, jumlah lapisan, serta arah tarik/titik pengunci. Konten membantu audience mengisolasi variabel sebelum mengganti seluruh gaya atau bahan.

## Audience problem / desire

Tepi hijab dapat berubah posisi ketika dipakai bergerak, berbicara, atau menoleh. Audience membutuhkan pemeriksaan visual yang singkat, tidak menyalahkan bentuk wajah, dan tidak menjanjikan satu teknik cocok untuk semua kain atau proporsi.

## Evidence boundary

- **Fakta:** tampilan dan perilaku tekstil dipengaruhi komposisi serat, benang, konstruksi, dan finishing; informasi produk dan care label tetap perlu diperiksa.
- **Observasi editorial:** garis awal, tumpukan lapisan, arah tarik, dan posisi pengunci dapat dibandingkan secara visual.
- **Hipotesis:** urutan tiga titik plus movement check dapat membantu audience mengenali variabel styling yang berubah.
- Bukan klaim medis, hasil produk, atau jaminan hijab tetap pada posisi tertentu.

## KPI / EXP-034

**Hypothesis:** a named three-point cheek-edge diagnostic plus a 30-second movement check will produce stronger completion, saves, and specific replies than a generic “add a pin” tip.

- **Variable:** three named checks + movement sequence.
- **Control:** single-tip Reel of comparable duration and visual quality, if tested later.
- **Primary KPI:** 3-second view rate and average watch time when Insights are available.
- **Secondary KPI:** completion, replays, saves, shares, profile visits, comments naming one check, Poll participation, and Questions replies.
- **Limitations:** face proportions, fabric construction/finish, inner, styling tension, attachment method, activity, and preference vary.
- **Result:** none; not published.

## Claim boundaries

- “Stay” describes visual position during wear, not a guaranteed property.
- A flatter starting edge and fewer stacked layers are styling variables, not universal rules.
- Avoid excessive tension and use pins/magnets/accessories only according to their instructions and personal comfort.
- AI imagery is editorial visualization, not a testimonial or documented before/after.
- No trend, product-performance, publication, audience-response, or account-performance claim is made.
