# DAY 24 — AI Visual and Motion Prompts

## Final ImageGen source prompt

**Use case:** `photorealistic-natural`  
**Asset type:** controlled three-panel editorial source for a vertical Instagram Reel about diagnosing why a pashmina shifts.

Create one highly realistic triptych with three equal vertical panels separated by narrow warm-ivory gutters. Show the exact same adult Southeast Asian Muslim woman, age 27–35, in the same studio, wearing the exact same opaque matte taupe pashmina and loose cocoa-brown abaya. Preserve identity, face, outfit, hijab color, camera, lighting, and overall drape.

- Panel 1: gently press the pashmina at crown/temple to check surface pairing; no inner edge or hair exposed.
- Panel 2: gently check a small discreet fabric-safe magnetic anchor at the layers below the chin; comfortable spacing, no sharp pin or tension.
- Panel 3: lightly hold the two pashmina tails near the upper torso to compare weight and length; full chest coverage.

Use realistic skin, hands, woven texture, folds, gravity, and opacity. Avoid text, logo, watermark, brands, exposed hair/neck/chest, transparent or tight clothing, sharp pins touching skin, painful tension, malformed hands, extra fingers/limbs, floating fabric, impossible folds, plastic skin, and synthetic AI appearance.

## Image-to-video / composition prompt

Create a deterministic 15-second, 1080 × 1920, 30 fps vertical composition from the still triptych. Use only a subtle 0–2.5% push-in. Do not synthesize lip movement, head movement, hand movement, or changing fabric state. Follow six scenes and exact frame counts: 72, 81, 81, 81, 72, 63. Use warm cream text cards, dark brown typography, dusty-rose accent, safe margins, and the on-screen copy from `COPYWRITING.md`.

## Motion boundary

- Still-image camera motion only.
- No fake demonstration of the pashmina physically sliding.
- No before/after proof, testimonial, or product result.
- Playhead-driven, deterministic, and seekable.
- No uncontrolled wall-clock animation, autoplay, random values, or network-dependent assets.
- No HyperFrames CLI result may be claimed unless a real runtime render is produced and verified.

## Reuse rules

- Split the source into three equal vertical panels without stretching.
- Preserve face, coverage, hand anatomy, magnet placement, fabric color, and lighting.
- Add audience-facing text only in the renderer.
- Keep planning labels out of the public visuals.
