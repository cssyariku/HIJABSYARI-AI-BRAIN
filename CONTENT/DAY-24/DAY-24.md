## Objective

Membantu audience mendiagnosis pashmina yang mudah bergeser melalui tiga titik yang dapat diuji satu per satu: pasangan permukaan kain–inner, posisi anchor bila digunakan, serta keseimbangan panjang dan drape.

## Audience problem / desire

Audience sering langsung menyalahkan bahan atau menambah banyak pin ketika pashmina bergeser. Padahal penyebab visual dapat berasal dari kombinasi beberapa faktor. Checklist singkat mengurangi trial-and-error dan mendorong perubahan yang nyaman, aman, serta mudah dibatalkan.

## Evidence boundary

- **Observasi visual:** perpindahan kain dapat dipengaruhi oleh interaksi permukaan, titik penahan, gerak tubuh, serta distribusi panjang/drape.
- **Hipotesis praktis untuk diuji:** mengubah satu faktor lalu melakukan tes gerak 30 detik membantu menemukan faktor yang paling relevan bagi pemakai.
- **Bukan fakta universal:** bahan, finishing, inner, gaya, aktivitas, bentuk kepala, dan preferensi kenyamanan berbeda.
- Tidak ada klaim tren, produk, material tertentu, atau hasil performa yang dibuat.

## KPI / EXP-025

**Hypothesis:** A three-point diagnostic plus a 30-second movement test will earn stronger completion, saves, and problem-specific comments than a single “add more pins” tip.  
**Variable:** ordered diagnosis: surface pairing → anchor → length/drape → test.  
**Control:** one-tip pashmina-stay Reel of comparable duration and visual quality, if tested later.  
**Primary KPI:** 3-second view rate and average watch time, when Insights are available.  
**Secondary KPI:** completion, replays, saves, shares, comments naming a problem location, profile visits, Poll participation, and Questions replies.  
**Limitations:** activity, fabric finishing, inner, head shape, anchor type, styling tension, and posting context can affect outcome.

No result is recorded until actual publication and Insights exist.

## Safety and use notes

- Change one factor at a time and keep styling comfortable.
- If using a pin or magnet, follow its product instructions; do not use damaged/sharp parts against skin.
- Do not tighten the pashmina until breathing, speaking, or jaw movement feels restricted.
- The visual anchor is an editorial illustration, not an endorsement of a specific product.
