# DAY 27 — AI Prompt Record

## ImageGen source prompt

Create a single high-resolution photorealistic editorial contact sheet for an Indonesian modest-fashion color comparison, with FIVE equal vertical panels in one wide composition. The SAME adult Southeast Asian Muslim woman (late 20s to early 30s) appears in every panel: identical face, natural realistic skin texture, identical calm expression, identical three-quarter standing pose, identical camera distance, identical soft daylight, identical warm off-white seamless studio background, identical loose ankle-length cream abaya with opaque long sleeves, identical simple styling. Her hijab is fully opaque and modest in every panel, covering all hair, ears, neck, shoulders and chest, with the same drape, pin placement, fabric texture and realistic folds. ONLY the hijab color changes from left to right: 1) light ivory beige, 2) soft warm peach beige, 3) soft dusty rose beige, 4) muted taupe beige, 5) deeper caramel beige. Preserve visibly distinct hue and depth differences without changing exposure or skin tone. Full body visible including hands and shoes; anatomically correct hands and fingers; realistic fabric gravity and hems; no jewelry, no handbag, no props. Premium approachable clean magazine photography, neutral color-managed lighting, high detail, realistic textile weave, natural proportions. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no watermark, no border labels, no collage decorations, no product packaging.

## Negative constraints

Avoid duplicated limbs, warped hands, plastic skin, floating fabric, sheer hijab, tight clothing, uncovered hair, ears, neck or chest, childlike model, glamour retouching, inconsistent face, pose, garment, lighting, scale, exposure, skin tone, or camera between panels.

## Composition notes

- The output is used as editorial source only; all public typography is added deterministically in `render.py`.
- The visual is not evidence of an exact seller shade, real product, customer experience, or universal color suitability.
- Panel names remain editorial descriptors because product naming is not standardized.
