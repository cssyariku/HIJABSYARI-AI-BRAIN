# DAY 20 — Research Notes and Claim Boundaries

Research checked 27 October 2026.

## Facts used
1. FTC care-label guidance requires garment care instructions to specify a satisfactory care method; washing instructions can cover hand/machine method and water temperature, while drying and ironing directions depend on what is safe for the garment.
2. The American Cleaning Institute advises following both fabric-care labels and detergent directions, notes that more detergent is not necessarily better, advises not overloading the washer, and suggests separating new/dark/bright items initially.
3. ACI recommends mesh bags for small/delicate items; the carousel presents this as an optional protective step, not a requirement for every hijab.
4. Textile-extension guidance states that overloading restricts movement and can reduce cleaning while increasing wear; delicate fabrics/knits may need gentler action.
5. Fiber-specific extension guidance demonstrates why one universal rule is inappropriate: some fibers can be washed, squeezed/blotted, blocked, drip-dried, or treated differently depending on fiber, construction, finish, and label.
