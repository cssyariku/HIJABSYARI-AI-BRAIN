## Objective

Menunjukkan lima arah warna hijab pada satu outfit denim yang dikontrol agar audience dapat membandingkan perubahan nuansa dengan cepat. Konten memberi referensi visual, bukan aturan warna universal.

## Audience problem / desire

Denim sering dipakai sebagai basic outfit, tetapi audience tetap dapat ragu memilih warna hijab selain biru atau hitam. Shade dan wash denim, cahaya, kamera, serta preferensi personal dapat mengubah hasil; karena itu Reel mengajak audience membandingkan pada outfitnya sendiri.

## Hook variants

1. Outfit denim + hijab apa? Coba 5 arah warna ini.
2. Denim nggak harus dipasangkan dengan hijab biru.
3. Satu outfit denim, lima nuansa berbeda.
4. Punya denim di lemari? Tes lima warna hijab ini.
5. Hijab netral atau berwarna untuk outfit denim?

**Selected hook:** “Outfit denim + hijab apa? Coba 5 arah warna ini.”  
**Rationale:** konteks dan manfaat langsung terbaca pada dua detik pertama tanpa klaim cocok untuk semua orang.

## Visual direction

- Controlled-variable editorial Color Lab.
- Exact same adult Indonesian/Southeast Asian Muslim woman, denim outfit, pose, crop, camera, background, and lighting.
- Only hijab color changes: soft ivory, warm taupe, dusty rose, muted olive, deep burgundy.
- Opaque matte fabric, realistic weave and gravity, full hair/ear/neck/chest coverage.
- Warm ivory layout with denim-blue accent and strong mobile hierarchy.
- No small category, series, or production label above the cover headline.
- Essential text remains inside vertical-video safe areas.

## Image and motion production

See `PROMPTS.md`. The source was generated with ImageGen. The HyperFrames skill was used to define deterministic, seekable scene timing, motion vocabulary, safe text and render checks. No HyperFrames CLI/runtime was installed in the production environment, so the final video was rendered deterministically from exact scene PNGs using Pillow and FFmpeg; no HyperFrames CLI output is claimed.
