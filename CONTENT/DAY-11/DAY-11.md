## Objective

Mengumpulkan preferensi audience tentang pashmina versus hijab segi empat, lalu memahami alasan di balik pilihan itu untuk membentuk topik konten berikutnya. Ini bukan lomba untuk menentukan jenis hijab yang paling benar.

## Audience problem / desire

Audience sering memilih bentuk hijab berdasarkan kombinasi rutinitas, kenyamanan, bahan, framing, dan aktivitas. Poll biner memudahkan respons awal; Questions menangkap alasan yang tidak dapat dijelaskan hanya oleh dua opsi.

## Hook variants

1. Kalau harus pilih satu: pashmina atau segi empat?
2. Tim drape panjang atau lipatan klasik?
3. Yang paling sering kamu ambil pagi-pagi: pashmina atau segi empat?
4. Satu minggu, satu pilihan—kamu tim yang mana?
5. Pilihan hijab harianmu lebih sering jatuh ke mana?

**Selected hook:** “Kalau harus pilih satu: pashmina atau segi empat?”  
**Rationale:** langsung, mudah dipahami, dan menurunkan friction untuk Poll tanpa menyiratkan pilihan benar atau salah.

## Frame script and shot list

Duration is a publishing guide, not a rendered video duration.

## Visual direction

- Warm ivory editorial studio, clean and approachable.
- The same adult Indonesian-presenting Muslim woman, cream abaya, consistent camera and lighting.
- Pashmina: warm taupe, elongated soft drape, full chest coverage.
- Segi empat: cool greige, neat triangular front, full chest coverage.
- Realistic fabric weave, skin, hands, and gravity.
- Strong mobile hierarchy and restrained neutral palette.
- No small category, series, or production label above the headline.
- Blank/native sticker zones stay inside Instagram-safe areas.

## KPI and measurement

**Primary KPI:** Poll votes and Poll participation rate, when Reach/Views denominator is available.  
**Secondary KPI:** Questions responses, completion from frame 1 to frame 4, exits, and replies.  
**Research fields:** preferred shape, reason, activity context, material mentioned, recurring problem language.

Do not compare Poll taps and Questions responses as equivalent actions. Do not infer audience preference before native Insights are supplied.

## Experiment tag — EXP-012

**Hypothesis:** A direct identity-choice Poll followed by an open Questions prompt produces both low-friction preference data and richer qualitative reasons.  
**Variable:** interaction sequence: binary Poll → context → Questions.  
**Control:** no established same-topic control yet.  
**Metrics:** Poll participation, Questions responses, completion, exits.  
**Limitation:** one Stories sequence cannot establish causality; placement and active audience size may affect results.
