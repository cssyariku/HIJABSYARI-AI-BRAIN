# DAY 11 — Visual Prompts

## ImageGen source prompt

**Use case:** `photorealistic-natural`  
**Asset type:** source editorial image for four Instagram Stories about choosing pashmina or square hijab  
**Scene:** warm ivory minimalist studio, seamless background, soft shadows  
**Subject:** the same adult Indonesian/Southeast Asian Muslim woman shown twice, waist-up, calm friendly expression; left wears a matte warm taupe pashmina with elongated drape and full chest coverage; right wears a matte cool greige square hijab folded into a neat triangular front with full chest coverage  
**Wardrobe:** identical modest long-sleeved cream abaya; no visible hair, ears, neck, or chest; no jewelry  
**Style:** natural premium editorial photography, realistic skin and believable fabric weave  
**Composition:** vertical split diptych, balanced scale and pose, clear negative space for text  
**Constraints:** same identity, outfit, camera and lighting; only hijab silhouette/color changes; no text, logo, or watermark  
**Avoid:** exposed coverage, transparent fabric, tight clothing, malformed hands, floating fabric, impossible folds, props, typography, glamour retouching

## Deterministic layout prompt

Render at 1080 × 1920. Use warm ivory, ink brown, warm taupe and cool greige. Prioritize one message per frame, high contrast, generous margins, and native-sticker safe areas. Do not display a small category/series/production label above any headline. Exact output is produced by `render.py`.
