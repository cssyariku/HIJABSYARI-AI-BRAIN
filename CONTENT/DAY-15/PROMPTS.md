# DAY 15 — AI Prompts

## Editorial source image prompt
Create a softly lit, high-quality indoor editorial portrait for an Indonesian hijab education carousel. Feature one adult Southeast Asian Muslim woman wearing an opaque cream hijab with full hair, ear, neck and chest coverage, a modest light top and an oversized loose blue denim shirt. Natural makeup, subtle smile, relaxed clasped hands, realistic anatomy and textile folds. Place the subject on the right side of a pale cream background with gentle window-shaped shadows and ample clean negative space on the left. Premium but approachable editorial photography, natural skin texture, soft side lighting, no text, no logo, no watermark, no exposed hair or neck, no tight clothing, no beauty-filter look.

## Carousel visual/layout prompt
Design an eight-slide Instagram educational carousel, 1080 × 1350, for Hijab Syari Indonesia. Topic: “Undertone 101: Mulai pilih warna hijab dari mana?” Use a clean editorial grid, warm cream background, restrained dusty-rose, olive, taupe and blue-grey accents, high-contrast dark typography, generous margins, one key message per slide, clear color swatches, and mobile-first text. Keep warm/cool/neutral as exploratory directions, not universal suitability rules. Do not place small category, series or production labels above the main cover headline. No fake logos or tiny decorative copy.

## Slide-specific palette prompts
- Warm direction: four flat textile swatches labeled camel/beige hangat, terracotta/bata, olive/hijau zaitun, cream hangat.
- Cool direction: mauve/dusty rose, berry/burgundy, blue-grey, putih dingin.
- Neutral direction: taupe, soft rose, sage lembut, beige seimbang.
Use the same swatch size, light and grid across slides for a controlled visual system. Swatches are visual examples only.

## Story prompt
Create three vertical Instagram Stories at 1080 × 1920 using the same cream editorial system. Story 1 is a low-friction Poll about choosing hijab by outfit color or personal preference. Story 2 is a three-step color comparison checklist. Story 3 asks which hijab color the audience finds difficult to style and reserves clear space for the native Questions sticker. Do not bake Instagram Poll/Questions UI into the PNG.

## Motion prompt
Not applicable: primary format is a static carousel and Stories. Keep any future animation subtle and do not morph face, fabric, hijab color, or body proportions.
