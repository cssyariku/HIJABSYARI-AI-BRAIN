## 1. PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT

### Product
Hijab Syari Indonesia (`@hijabsyaricoid`) adalah media digital hijab/modest-fashion Indonesia berbasis konten, bukan toko pada fase reaktivasi.

### Problem
Akun lama tidak aktif sehingga:
- distribusi dan kebiasaan audience perlu dibangun ulang;
- belum diketahui content-market fit terkini;
- belum ada sistem produksi stabil;
- tidak tersedia model/manpower creator yang besar;
- monetisasi terlalu dini berpotensi mengurangi trust.

### Solution
Bangun sistem media **AI-first + faceless** yang memproduksi edukasi, solusi, inspirasi, discovery, dan interaksi komunitas secara konsisten. AI mempercepat produksi; keputusan editorial, verifikasi, taste, dan evaluasi tetap harus dikendalikan secara kritis.

### Vision
Menjadi akun yang dicari muslimah Indonesia ketika ingin:
- memahami hijab;
- menyelesaikan masalah styling;
- memilih warna/material;
- mendapatkan inspirasi modest look;
- memahami tren tanpa harus mengikuti semua tren;
- menemukan rekomendasi yang relevan dan transparan.

### Core promise
**“Bikin urusan hijab lebih mudah, menarik, dan inspiratif.”**

Setiap konten minimal harus melakukan satu:
**TEACH / SOLVE / INSPIRE / DISCOVER / ENTERTAIN / CONNECT.**

## 4. AUDIENCE

### Primary
Muslimah Indonesia, terutama Gen Z dan millennial, mobile-first, tertarik pada hijab/modest fashion dan solusi praktis.

### Persona A — Hijab Beginner
Butuh dasar: material, inner, warna, cara menjaga bentuk, basic styling.

### Persona B — Daily Hijaber
Butuh solusi cepat untuk kampus, kantor, aktivitas harian.

### Persona C — Modest Fashion Explorer
Mencari kombinasi warna, outfit, tren, inspiration board.

### Persona D — Problem Solver
Datang karena problem konkret: hijab maju, licin, menerawang, kusut, warna membuat wajah terlihat kusam, dll.

### Audience guardrails
Jangan mengasumsikan semua audience:
- memiliki face shape/skin tone/undertone sama;
- punya budget sama;
- memiliki tingkat religiositas yang sama;
- membutuhkan gaya yang sama.

Hindari body/face shaming. Styling advice adalah opsi visual, bukan penilaian harga diri.

## 9. AI-FIRST PRODUCTION

Workflow:

**Research → Opportunity → Hook → Script → AI Visual → Edit/Layout → Caption → QA → Publish → Measure → Learn**

AI dapat digunakan untuk:
- editorial hijab model;
- outfit/color visualization;
- educational illustration;
- infographic;
- carousel visual;
- background;
- image-to-video;
- ide/script/caption;
- voiceover bila sesuai.

### AI transparency/ethics
Dilarang menggunakan AI untuk:
- fake customer testimonial;
- fake “saya sudah memakai produk ini”;
- fake before/after product results;
- fabricated celebrity endorsement;
- fabricated documentary/news evidence.

AI model harus diperlakukan sebagai **editorial visualization**.

## 12. 90-DAY ROADMAP

### Days 1–30 — REACTIVATE & LEARN
Goals:
- re-establish posting consistency;
- test pillars;
- establish baseline;
- discover first winners;
- test AI production system.

Do not set arbitrary absolute reach targets before enough data exists.

Test:
- problem vs curiosity hooks;
- carousel length;
- Reel duration;
- covers;
- color vs problem topics;
- AI editorial vs graphic-heavy visual;
- CTA.

### Days 31–60 — SCALE WINNERS
After enough evidence:
- ~70% proven patterns;
- ~20% variations;
- ~10% experiments.

Build recurring IP:
- Hijab Fix #xx
- Hijab Color Lab #xx
- Hijab Mistakes #xx
- Hijab 101 #xx
- Match Your Hijab #xx

### Days 61–90 — BUILD AUTHORITY
Build evergreen clusters:
- Color Guide
- Material Guide
- Face & Style Guide
- Hijab Care Guide
- Occasion Guide

Turn isolated posts into searchable knowledge ecosystem.

## 13. KPI PHILOSOPHY

Tier 1 — Discovery:
- Reach
- Non-follower Reach
- Reels Views
- Profile Visits

Tier 2 — Utility/Quality:
- Saves
- Shares
- Save Rate
- Share Rate
- retention/watch time when available

Tier 3 — Growth:
- Follows from Content
- Follow Conversion
- Follower Growth
- Returning audience signals when available

Do not compare Reel views directly with carousel saves as if objective were identical.

Prefer:
- median;
- same-format comparison;
- same-objective comparison;
- repeated evidence.

## 14. MONETIZATION GATE

Monetization should follow:

**Education → Recommendation → Curation → Affiliate/Commerce**

Do not start affiliate merely because follower count reaches an arbitrary number.

Signals that monetization can be tested:
- stable posting system;
- recurring audience;
- proven content IP;
- audience asks product/material recommendations;
- recommendation can be integrated naturally;
- engagement is not dependent on giveaways/random virality.

Potential categories:
- hijab;
- inner;
- magnets/pins/accessories;
- hijab care;
- modest outfits;
- storage/care tools;
- marketplace curation;
- sponsored educational content;
- brand collaboration.

Any commercial relationship should be transparently disclosed.

---

## 15. DECISION RULES

1. Trust beats short-term conversion.
2. Specificity beats generic advice.
3. Utility beats filler.
4. Shareability/saveability beat vanity metrics for education.
5. Strong hooks are welcome; deceptive hooks are not.
6. One viral post is not strategy.
7. Content IP beats random posting.
8. AI improves production efficiency, not truth.
9. Recommendations must remain useful without affiliate links.
10. Strategy changes must be recorded in CURRENT_STATE and CHANGELOG.

---

## 16. LONG-TERM BUSINESS MODEL

**MEDIA → AUDIENCE → COMMUNITY → TRUST → RECOMMENDATION → AFFILIATE → BRAND PARTNERSHIP → OPTIONAL OWN COMMERCE**

Commerce is an extension of media trust, not the foundation of the account.
