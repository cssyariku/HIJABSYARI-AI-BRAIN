### EXP-005
Test cover specificity:

- B: specific “5 Warna Hijab yang Gampang Dipakai dengan Outfit Hitam”

### EXP-006
Hypothesis: a binary A/B Poll lowers response friction compared with an open-ended Questions prompt. Treat the two actions as different interaction types and do not infer a winner from one sequence.

### EXP-007
Hypothesis: an affirmative, options-based face-framing hook can preserve discovery utility without corrective or face-shaming language. Compare only against reasonably similar Face & Style Reels.

### EXP-008
Hypothesis: a decision framework plus pre-purchase checklist produces stronger saves than a material comparison that is only descriptive. Compare against similar Hijab 101 carousels.

### EXP-009
Hypothesis: a controlled-variable Color Lab—same model, outfit, pose, and lighting with only hijab color changing—produces stronger save/share response than mixed-look inspiration.

### EXP-010
Hypothesis: a numbered five-look inspiration sequence with a short contrarian hook can improve discovery and completion compared with a single-look inspiration Reel of similar duration and visual quality.

### EXP-011
Hypothesis: a three-source troubleshooting checklist produces stronger save/share response than a single-tip Hijab Fix carousel of comparable visual quality.

### EXP-012
Hypothesis: a direct identity-choice Poll followed by an open Questions prompt produces both low-friction preference data and richer qualitative reasons. Poll and Questions remain different interaction types.

### EXP-013
Hypothesis: a controlled four-color sequence plus a practical at-home test earns stronger completion and saves than a single-look Color Lab Reel. No result is recorded yet.

### EXP-014
Hypothesis: a four-factor diagnostic checklist plus a 30-second pre-wear test earns more saves and problem-specific comments than generic advice to choose thicker fabric. No result is recorded yet.

### EXP-015
Hypothesis: a rapid five-color controlled comparison for a familiar denim context can encourage rewatches and saves compared with single-look denim inspiration. No result is recorded yet.

### EXP-016
Hypothesis: a warm/cool/neutral framework paired with palette swatches and a simple at-home comparison will earn stronger saves than a color list without context. No result is recorded yet.

### EXP-017
Hypothesis: a controlled three-option framing Reel with named looks and an A/B/C recap will produce stronger completion, saves, and preference comments than a single-look Face & Style Reel. No result is recorded yet.

### EXP-018
Hypothesis: five complete outfit–hijab pairings with short visual descriptors and a three-question chooser will earn stronger saves and shares than a swatch-only earth-tone guide. No result is recorded yet.

Do not change multiple major variables simultaneously when trying to isolate causality.
